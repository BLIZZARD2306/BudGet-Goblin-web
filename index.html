Features

Everything a budget needs. Nothing it doesn't.

No spreadsheets. Just your money, where it went, and what's left.

Follows your payday

Monthly, twice a month, every 2 weeks or weekly. Your remaining budget resets when you get paid.

A wallet for every card

Cash, e-wallets, debit and credit cards, each with its own balance and a full history that adds up.

See where it went

Spending by category, income vs. spent, a 20% savings goal and a goblin report on the biggest bite.

Split with friends

Enter the total and how many people. Only your share is logged.

Synced across phones

Your budget follows you to every phone you sign in on, and a dot shows when it's up to date.

No passwords

Sign in with Google or an email link. Peso, dollar, euro or pound. Light or dark.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

BudGet installs straight from this page, no Play Store needed.

Download

Tap Download on your phone. The file is about 20 MB.

Open the file

Open it from your notifications or Downloads. If asked, allow your browser to install apps.

Meet your goblin

Set your payday and budget. From then on, BudGet updates itself when you open it.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

BudGet is shared directly from here. Google Play Protect may ask to scan it the first time, since it didn't come from the Play Store. That's normal.

How do updates work?

When you open BudGet, it checks for a new version and offers to download and install it. The first time, Android asks you to let BudGet install apps.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Each account can read and write its own data and nothing else. Deleting your account removes everything from the cloud.

Do I need an account?

Signing in with Google or an email link lets BudGet sync across your phones. Opened the email on a computer? Type the 6-digit code the app shows and your phone signs in.
